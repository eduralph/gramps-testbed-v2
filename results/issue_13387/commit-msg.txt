Fix date.get_start_stop_range() overpadding compound estimated dates

Date.get_start_stop_range() applies the ±"about" preference padding
(behavior.date-about-range, default 50 years) when quality is estimated,
but this incorrectly widens explicitly bounded compound dates (MOD_RANGE /
MOD_SPAN — e.g. "estimated between 1968 and 1978"). An explicit "between"
defines its own range; the approximation window applies only when the bounds
are not explicit. This fix conditions the estimated-quality padding on
is_compound(), so only non-compound estimated dates receive the padding. Single
estimated dates (MOD_NONE) still get padded as before. The fix is verified by
regression tests in date_test.py that confirm compound ranges keep their explicit
bounds while single-estimated dates remain padded.

Fixes #13387

Distinguish adopted relationships from step relationships

The relationship calculator previously collapsed every non-birth link
to "step", so an adopted child was reported as a stepchild despite
Gramps recording the relation explicitly as ChildRefType.ADOPTED. This
change threads the ChildRefType through the relationship paths so the
calculator can distinguish an adopted qualifier from "step", and
introduces an adopted sibling type so siblings of an adopted child are
correctly related through their adoptive parent.

The fix introduces new path codes (REL_MOTHER_ADOPT, REL_FATHER_ADOPT,
REL_FAM_ADOPT) and a sibling type (ADOPT_SIB), carries the ChildRefType
to the naming decision point, and adds helper methods to detect adopted
parent lists. The shared "adopted" qualifier is a single localizable
choice (matching the brief's design goal), and non-adopted behavior is
byte-for-byte unchanged—genuine stepchildren still render as "step".

Adopted relationships now render as "adopted son/father/brother" across
all relationship consumers (Narrative Web, relationship view, reports)
where they were incorrectly called "stepson/stepfather/stepbrother"
before.

Fixes #10554

Add regression test for Top Surnames wrong-surname bug

Issue #6826 reported that the Top Surnames gramplet selected a wrong
representative for surnames, causing the Same Surnames quick view to open
reports for a different surname than the one clicked. The root cause was
unconditional representative assignment during iteration, favoring the
last-iterated person regardless of whether the clicked surname was their
primary or alternate name.

This fix was already applied by commit e39dc09e2e, which refactored
record_surnames() to prefer representatives whose primary surname matches
the group name. This test verifies the reported scenario — a person with
primary surname "A" plus alternate "B", and another with primary "B" —
now correctly resolves "B" to the primary-B carrier regardless of
iteration order. The test also confirms the fix remains stable across
future refactors of representative selection.

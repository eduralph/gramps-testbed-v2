Fix patronymic surname fragmentation in gramplet counts

The Surname Cloud and Statistics gramplets counted each patronymic
variant as a separate surname entry, so people sharing "Иванов" with
different patronymics ("Петрович", "Сергеевич") tallied as three
surnames instead of one. Root cause: the gramplets enumerated the
full multi-component surname string (via Name.get_surname()) without
filtering origin, so patronymic components inflated the count.

This change introduces a shared counting routine
(get_counting_surname) that drops non-primary surname components of
patronymic or matronymic origin before formatting, leaving every
other secondary surname (e.g., a Spanish maternal name) untouched.
Both gramplets route through it to collapse patronymic noise while
preserving legitimate secondary family names.

Fixes #6988
